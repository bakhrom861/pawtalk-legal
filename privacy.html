Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

PawTalk ("the app", "we") is an entertainment app that turns your pet's sounds and photos into funny, made-up translations. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: a recording or photo is sent to our server only to make the translation, and neither we nor our AI provider keep it.

AI translation and your consent

Before the app sends anything to the AI for the first time, it asks for your permission. If you choose "Use without AI", nothing is uploaded and translations are made on your device. You can turn AI translation on or off at any time in Profile.

Microphone recordings

When you tap the record button, the app records up to 10 seconds of sound. To make the translation, the recording is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to an AI model (Google Gemini, accessed through the kie.ai service) together with your pet's name, species, sex, personality, the name your pet calls you (if you entered one), the app language and the hour of day. The answer comes back as text. Our server does not save the recording; it only counts how many translations each installation made, to apply daily limits. On your phone, each new recording overwrites the previous one. If the server cannot be reached, the app makes a translation on the device instead.

Photos and camera

Photos you take or choose are stored inside the app on your device. A profile photo is never uploaded. A photo you use for "Read their mind" is sent to our server and the AI model in the same way as a recording, only to make that translation, and is not saved there. The app only sees the photos you pick.

Pet profile and history

Your pet's name, species, personality and your translation history are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. Our server keeps only a random installation identifier and a count of translations; the count records are deleted after 60 days.

Feedback

If you rate the app 1 to 3 stars in the app, you can send us a short note. We receive the number of stars, the topics you picked, the text you wrote, the app language and the random installation identifier. We use it only to improve the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Adapty (adapty.io) to check whether your subscription is active. Adapty receives an anonymous app profile identifier, your purchase receipts and basic device information (such as iOS version and country). It does not receive your recordings, photos or pet data. See Adapty's privacy policy at adapty.io/privacy.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No push token is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not create accounts or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell data or use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

PawTalk is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your choices

You can turn off microphone, camera, photo and notification access at any time in iOS Settings. You can delete a pet and its translations inside the app, or delete all data by removing the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
